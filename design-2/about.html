Our Story

Started small. Still doing it ourselves.

Reece launched this business after years of watching property maintenance companies send whoever was available — and watching quality suffer for it. He wanted to do things differently.

The idea was simple: be the person who shows up. Be the person who actually knows what the job needs. Be accountable for the result.

"I started this so I could do the work right, not so I could manage people cutting corners."

— Reece, Founder & Owner

Five years in, that approach hasn't changed. Reece still handles every job personally. That's not a marketing line — it's how the business was built, and it's what keeps customers coming back.
