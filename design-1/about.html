Our Story

Started with one truck and a commitment to doing it right.

Reece didn't build this company to dispatch other people. He built it to do the work himself — to show up on time, take pride in every cut, and leave every property better than he found it.

What followed was a reputation. Neighbor to neighbor, yard by yard. Word travels fast when someone actually cares about the results instead of just the schedule.

Today we serve homeowners across Eden Prairie, Minnetonka, Burnsville, and the surrounding Twin Cities. The operation stays intentionally tight — because quality only holds when the person accountable is the one doing the work.
